Billboard – Component test

This page tests billboard variants: --image (normal + reverse), --text, and themes --light/--dark. Resize the viewport to cross 768px to verify grid layout changes.

What to check:
• At < 768px, the layout stacks in one column (media then content).
• At ≥ 768px, billboard--image becomes two columns, and billboard--reverse swaps content/media order.
• billboard--image content should have a top divider line (pseudo-element) and padding-top.
• billboard__content maxes at ~60ch and doesn’t overflow.
• Link and button styles (inline-flex, gap, border) render correctly across themes.
