Download links – Component test

This page tests .zh-download-link structure, the left colour bar, ellipsis behavior, meta layout, right-side icon area, hover/focus states, and visited-link styling (:visited inherits).

How to test:
• Hover: background should change to --uni-blue10.
• Focus: use Tab to see highlight outline (also verify :focus-visible).
• Title truncation: long titles should ellipsis (nowrap + overflow hidden + text-overflow).
• Layout: left bar sits flush to edge; right area has a left border and stays aligned.
• Visited: after clicking, the link should not turn purple/blue—should stay color: inherit.
