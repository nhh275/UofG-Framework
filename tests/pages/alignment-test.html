Vertical-align Utilities

These utilities apply to inline and inline-block elements only. Alignment is calculated relative to the text baseline and the line box, not the visual height of adjacent elements.

baseline / top / middle/ bottom

baseline, top, middle, and bottom align elements relative to the line box. middle aligns to the baseline plus half the x-height (it is not visual centering).

Baseline Top Middle Bottom
text-top / text-bottom

text-top and text-bottom align elements to the parent’s font metrics (em box), not to neighbouring elements. Results may appear subtle when mixed with non-text content.
